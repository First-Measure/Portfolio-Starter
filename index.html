02THE PROBLEM

Most professional experience is trapped in documents.

A résumé records where you've been. It rarely has room to explain the thinking, craft, judgment, creativity, leadership, or impact behind the work.

Your portfolio should do more than repeat your résumé. It should give people a reason to understand your work — and remember you.
